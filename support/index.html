Can I buy chips or win prizes?

No. The chips are free and have no cash value. You cannot buy chips, win prizes, or exchange chips for money or anything else.

Where is my game data?

Only on your phone. Chiptopia has no accounts and no server, so we cannot restore or move your chips. When you delete the app, the phone deletes this data too.

How do I report an ad?

In the app, open Profile, then Report an ad: your mail app opens an email to us. You can also write to the address above.

How do I change my ad choices?

Where the law gives you these choices, open Profile, then Privacy choices. On an iPhone, you can also change the permission to track in the settings: Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Why does the app ask for my birth month?

Chiptopia is only for adults. The app keeps only the result on your phone, never the date.
