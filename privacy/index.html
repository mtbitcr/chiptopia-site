Data on your phone

Your chip balance, levels, missions, achievements, glass collection, detailed game history and settings stay on your phone. Chiptopia has no server and no accounts, so we do not receive this data. When you delete the app, the phone deletes this data too.

If your phone makes backups (to your Google account, iCloud or a computer), a backup holds a copy of this data. The backup belongs to your account; we do not receive it.

The age question

Chiptopia is only for adults. At the first start, it asks for your birth month and year. It keeps only the result on your phone, never the date: that you are 18 or over, or the month from which you can answer again.

Ads

Chiptopia is free because it shows ads. The ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. To show and measure ads, the Google software in the app processes data such as your IP address, your approximate location (Google derives it from the IP address), information about your device, the advertising ID of your device, diagnostic data and your interactions with the ads. Google can process this data outside the EU, for example in the USA, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Chiptopia sends data only to Google. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google can share this data with the ad partners that the consent form lists. Most uses need your consent. Some partners use the data on the basis of their legitimate interest, and you can object to this in the form. Google and these partners give this data the same or equal protection as this policy.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Chiptopia asks for your consent with the consent form of Google before it loads ads. Personalised ads and the storage of data on your device need your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). Without it, Google can show limited ads, which use no personalisation and no storage on your device. For these, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in a free app that ads pay for (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The form keeps your choice on your phone. In some US states, the same form lets you object to the sale or sharing of your data.

On an iPhone, Chiptopia then asks for your permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). Without your permission, the app does not use the advertising ID of your iPhone. You can change this permission in the settings of your iPhone: Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Usage statistics

On Android, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which games players try and how often they return. This is a service of Google Ireland Limited. The app sends Google an ID for this installation of the app, the screens that you open, the start and the length of your sessions, the app version, the model, the operating system and the language of your device. Google derives your country and region from your IP address; for players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics does not store the IP address. The statistics use no advertising ID.

To measure the first game experience, the app records whether you start and complete your first round, and which game it is. These events include no chip balance, stake or payout. A record on your phone prevents the app from sending the same first-round event again after a restart.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app sends statistics only with your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021): in the consent form, your consent to "Store and/or access information on a device" is also your consent to the statistics. Without it, the app sends no statistics. If you withdraw your consent (Profile, then Privacy choices), the app stops the statistics and deletes the statistics data on your phone. In other countries, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in a better app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google processes the statistics for us. It keeps the events for 2 months and the data of each installation for 14 months. Google can process this data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

On Android, we link Analytics with our AdMob account. Google combines app usage statistics with ad impressions and the advertising revenue they generate. This helps us measure ad performance, improve the app and understand its advertising income.

News from Chiptopia

If you turn on News from Chiptopia in the profile, your phone shows at most 2 notifications a week with news about the app. For this, the app registers your phone with Firebase Cloud Messaging, a service of Google Ireland Limited. Google receives a push token and an ID for this installation of the app, the app version and technical data of your device, such as the operating system and the model. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). When you turn the news off, the app deletes the push token, so Google can no longer send news to your phone. You can also turn off the notifications in the settings of your phone. Google can process this data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Reminders

If you turn on the reminder for the daily bonus, your phone shows at most one notification a day: at 10:00 when the bonus is ready, and, if you do not open the app after it, once more at 12:30 on the next Saturday or Sunday. The phone plans them, and no data leaves your phone for them. You can turn them off in the lobby or in the settings of your phone.

Emails to us

If you write to us, for example to report an ad, we use your email address and your message only to answer you. We delete them when the matter is closed, unless the law requires us to keep them. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Our website

GitHub hosts our website with GitHub Pages. When you open a page of the website, GitHub logs your IP address and stores it for security purposes. We do not receive these logs. The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no other tracking. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub can process this data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
