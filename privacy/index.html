Data on your phone

Your chips, levels, missions, achievements, game statistics and settings stay on your phone. Chiptopia has no server and no accounts, so we do not receive this data. When you delete the app, the phone deletes this data too.

If your phone makes backups (to your Google account, iCloud or a computer), a backup holds a copy of this data. The backup belongs to your account; we do not receive it.

The age question

Chiptopia is only for adults. At the first start, it asks for your birth month and year. It keeps only the result on your phone, never the date: that you are 18 or over, or the month from which you can answer again.

Ads

Chiptopia is free because it shows ads. The ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. To show and measure ads, the Google software in the app processes data such as your IP address, your approximate location (Google derives it from the IP address), information about your device, the advertising ID of your device, diagnostic data and your interactions with the ads. Google can process this data outside the EU, for example in the USA, under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Chiptopia shares data only with Google, and Google gives this data the same or equal protection as this policy.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Chiptopia asks for your consent with the consent form of Google before it loads ads. Personalised ads and the storage of data on your device need your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021). Without it, Google can show limited ads, which use no personalisation and no storage on your device. For these, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in a free app that ads pay for (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The form keeps your choice on your phone. In some US states, the same form lets you object to the sale or sharing of your data.

On an iPhone, Chiptopia then asks for your permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). Without your permission, the app does not use the advertising ID of your iPhone. You can change this permission in the settings of your iPhone: Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

Where the law gives you these choices, you can change them at any time: Profile, then Privacy choices. More about the data that Google processes:

Reminders

If you turn on the reminder for the daily bonus, your phone shows at most one notification a day, at 10:00, when the bonus is ready. The phone plans it, and no data leaves your phone for it. You can turn it off in the lobby or in the settings of your phone.

Emails to us

If you write to us, for example to report an ad, we use your email address and your message only to answer you. We delete them when the matter is closed, unless the law requires us to keep them. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Our website

GitHub hosts our website with GitHub Pages. When you open a page of the website, GitHub logs your IP address and stores it for security purposes. We do not receive these logs. The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no other tracking. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub can process this data in the USA under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
